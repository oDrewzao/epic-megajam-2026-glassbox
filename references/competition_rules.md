# 2026 Epic AI MegaJam - Official Competition Reference

> **Quick Reference for AI Agents & Developers:** This document synthesizes all mandatory constraints, deadlines, scoring rubrics, and modifier requirements from the official 16-page rulebook (`https://itch.io/jam/2026-epic-ai-megajam`). All features and code authored across Antigravity, Cursor, Claude Code, or any other agent/tool must conform strictly to these rules.

---

## 1. Key Dates & Official Timezone

- **Official Jam Timezone:** **Eastern Time (ET / EST / UTC-5)**
- **Contest Start:** **November 12, 2026 at 12:00 AM ET** (Midnight)
- **Contest Deadline:** **November 15, 2026 at 8:59 PM ET**
- **Total Duration:** Exactly ~93 hours
- **All telemetry timestamps and milestone tracking MUST be recorded in Eastern Time (ET / UTC-5).**

---

## 2. Submission Mandates & Platform Requirements (UEFN Track)

- **Platform:** Unreal Editor for Fortnite (UEFN) / Fortnite Ecosystem.
- **Island Snapshot:** Must be submitted as a **Private Version Code** of the island created strictly within the contest period and playable in Fortnite.
  - *Critical Rule:* Live island codes and playtest codes are **NOT** accepted (they can be updated post-deadline). Only the fixed snapshot Private Version Code created before 8:59 PM ET on Nov 15 will be judged.
- **Gameplay Video:** Must include a short video between **30 and 60 seconds** demonstrating recorded gameplay, at **1920x1080 resolution or higher**.
- **Language Mandate:** **100% English** across all in-game text, UI, dialogue, audio VO, gameplay video, and entry materials (English-speaking judging panel).
- **Standalone Execution:** The game must not require any external software outside of Fortnite/UEFN to run.
- **Team Size:** Solo developer (1 entrant), competing for the **Solo Driver** modifier.
- **Pre-Jam Preparation Allowance (Section 7h):** Entrants are explicitly permitted to prepare AI tools, agent configurations, Model Context Protocol (MCP) settings, prompt libraries, and scripts before the Contest Period. All game content (gameplay mechanics, levels, and art assets) must be created strictly during the Contest Period.

---

## 3. Core Scoring Rubric (35 Points Max)

Judged on a 5-point scale across 7 criteria:
1. **Ambition and Innovation (Up to 5 pts):** Scored on the submission itself. Real-time reality editing via diegetic Director's Remote.
2. **Use of Contest Theme (Up to 5 pts):** Integration of the official theme: **"DIRECTOR'S CUT"**.
3. **Fun Factor (Up to 5 pts):** Moment-to-moment game feel, responsiveness, replayability. *(Crucial: Used as the official tie-breaker with double weight!)*
4. **Gameplay (Up to 5 pts):** Polish of combat, hazards, controls, and roguelite progression loop.
5. **Visuals (Up to 5 pts):** Diegetic lighting, cinematic staging, cohesive movie set aesthetic.
6. **Level Design (Up to 5 pts):** Constellation arena layouts, spatial pacing, cover, and hazard placement.
7. **Audio (Up to 5 pts):** Diegetic SFX (tape rewind, scissors cut, clapperboards), atmospheric cues, voice acting / radio lines.

*Note on AI Disclosure:* The extent of AI use disclosed will be considered for context only; it will not on its own increase or decrease the score.

---

## 4. Target Modifier Categories

Our project specifically targets **3 modifier awards**:
1. **Glass Box:** Awarded for the best devlog documenting how the entrant created their submission.
   - *Requirement:* The devlog must be submitted together with the submission during the contest period (via itch.io / GitHub-backed live devlog).
2. **Walkie-Talkie:** Awarded for the best narrative-driven UEFN game featuring Large Language Model (LLM)-powered NPCs built with the UEFN conversations feature.
   - *Requirement:* Interactive radio director (**Stanley**) reacting contextually in English to the player's run.
3. **Solo Driver:** Awarded for the best game created by a single developer.

---

## 5. Content & IP Guardrails

- **Age Rating:** Content suitable for players aged 13+ (consistent with ESRB "Teen" / Fortnite standards).
- **Trademarks & Logos:** No unauthorized third-party trademarks or logos. UEFN submissions may use the official "Developed in Fortnite" branding per Epic guidelines.
- **Sourced Assets (Section 15c):** Any non-original / sourced assets must be noted upon submission. Content generated with AI tools during the jam is considered original, not sourced.
- **Security & Privacy:** Redact all Epic Account IDs, private version codes, and API keys in public repositories and telemetry logs.
